- Base: https://brand.base.org/core-identifiers and https://github.com/base/brand-kit — the official digital Square asset is retained in `public/brands/base-square.svg`.
- Ethereum: https://ethereum.org/en/assets/ — diamond identifier used alongside Ethereum ecosystems.
- Solana: https://solana.com/branding — three parallel ribbons identify the Solana section.

- Xiaomi MiMo: https://github.com/XiaomiMiMo/MiMo — model-family naming.

Brand identifiers are used for navigation and context. Relationship labels and descriptions are supplied separately from `data/relationships.ts`; their display does not imply employment, partnership, or endorsement.
